What is astrocartography

Your birth chart, drawn on the world map

Astrocartography takes the sky of your birth and projects each planet onto the Earth. The result is a set of lines that cross countries and cities, and each one carries the energy of a planet.

Near a Venus line, love and beauty tend to flow. Under Jupiter, doors open. In other places the energy asks more of you, and teaches you more too. There is no good or bad city: there is the right city for the moment you are living.

That is why some people feel at home in a place they have never been, and others never quite fit in the city where they were born.

FAQ

Before you board

Do I need my birth time?

Yes. The time matters, because the lines move with the hour. Ideally use the time on your birth certificate. If you do not have the exact time, use the closest one you know.

How do I get access?

Right after checkout, access is unlocked for the email you used to pay, and you get an email with the link. Sign in with that email and open your map. It stays available whenever you want to come back.

How does the compatibility % work?

For every city, the map weighs which of your planetary lines pass nearby, how close they are and how strongly they touch each theme. That becomes a score from 1 to 99%. You can rank the whole world or only your home country, overall or by theme (love, career, healing…).

Does astrocartography predict the future?

No. It shows tendencies: the kind of energy each place tends to awaken in you. The choice is always yours. The map is a compass, not a verdict.

Is it useful if I cannot travel right now?

Yes. Many people use the map to understand their relationship with the city they live in or grew up in, or to plan a future move calmly.

Do I need to know astrology?

No. The map translates the lines into everyday themes (love, work, money, wellbeing) so anyone can explore it on their own.
